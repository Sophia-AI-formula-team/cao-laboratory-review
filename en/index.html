Cao Laboratory

Control Engineering at Sophia University

We develop control systems for autonomous vehicles, automotive powertrains, mobile robots, and EV-based energy delivery. Our work aims to improve safety, efficiency, and mobility.

View research areasOfficial faculty profile ↗

What we study

  • Autonomous vehicle motion control
  • Automotive powertrain control
  • Autonomous mobile robots
  • EV energy delivery and mobility planning
HOW WE WORK
We build mathematical models, run computer simulations, and test control methods with vehicles and robots.
Location
Yotsuya Campus, Tokyo

About the laboratory

Control theory applied to real systems

We begin by describing how a system moves and responds with a mathematical model. We then design a controller, test it in simulation and experiments, and apply what we learn to vehicles, powertrains, mobile robots, and energy-delivery systems.
