Applied research program

AI Formula Research Program

05

Research and development toward AI Formula using an autonomous vehicle platform for integrated control-system testing.

Status
Active program
Period
Ongoing
Main topics
Autonomous vehicles · Control systems · System integration

Overview

What we study

AI Formula provides an applied setting for connecting autonomous-vehicle sensing, planning, and control. Sophia University's 2026 science and technology report records joint research with Honda R&D toward the AI Formula program during the 2025 academic year.

Problem we are addressing

An integrated autonomous platform must keep multiple subsystems synchronized and must produce evidence that engineers can use to explain and improve vehicle behavior.

Approach

How we work

  1. A

    Integrate

    Connect sensing, vehicle-state estimation, planning, and low-level control through explicit interfaces.

  2. B

    Test

    Use recorded and physical tests to evaluate each subsystem and the complete control loop.

  3. C

    Review

    Keep run data and engineering notes so that failures can be traced to concrete system behavior.

  4. D

    Iterate

    Turn test evidence into scoped changes and verify them against the same repeatable process.

Research directions

Research directions

  • An applied platform for autonomous-vehicle control research
  • A shared integration space for student project teams
  • Repeatable evidence from simulation, recorded data, and physical tests
