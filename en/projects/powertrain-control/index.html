Research area

Powertrain and Energy-Efficient Control

02

Optimal control of automotive powertrains to improve fuel economy, emissions, and driving comfort.

Status
Active research
Period
Ongoing
Main topics
Powertrain control · Energy efficiency · Mode optimization

Overview

What we study

Powertrain control determines how a vehicle produces and delivers driving force. The laboratory studies optimal operation and switching strategies that account for travel conditions while balancing efficiency, emissions, and comfort.

Problem we are addressing

Improving one operating metric can make another worse. A practical controller must balance fuel or energy use with emissions, responsiveness, and ride quality over an entire journey.

Approach

How we work

  1. A

    Characterize operation

    Model how powertrain components behave across operating modes and driving conditions.

  2. B

    Predict the journey

    Use available route and traffic information to evaluate decisions beyond the immediate time step.

  3. C

    Optimize switching

    Choose operating modes and control inputs that satisfy multiple performance goals.

  4. D

    Compare trade-offs

    Measure efficiency, emissions, and comfort together rather than optimizing a single isolated score.

Research directions

Research directions

  • Journey-aware powertrain control formulations
  • Operating-mode strategies for hybrid and conventional vehicle systems
  • Quantified trade-offs among efficiency, emissions, and comfort
Next step

Future studies will continue to incorporate more realistic traffic and operating uncertainty into controller evaluation.
