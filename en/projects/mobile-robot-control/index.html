Research area

Autonomous Mobile Robot Control

03

Autonomous navigation and control for mobile robots, including sensor-equipped electric wheelchairs.

Status
Active research
Period
Ongoing
Main topics
Robot control · Path planning · Obstacle avoidance

Overview

What we study

The laboratory studies how mobile robots can plan routes, follow them accurately, and avoid obstacles. An electric-wheelchair platform has been used to connect sensing, path planning, and vehicle control in a practical mobility setting.

Problem we are addressing

Robots that share space with people must cope with limited sensing and environments that change after a route has been planned.

Approach

How we work

  1. A

    Sense the environment

    Use onboard sensors to represent obstacles and the robot's local operating space.

  2. B

    Plan a route

    Generate a feasible route from a selected starting point to a destination.

  3. C

    Follow and correct

    Control the platform along the planned route while correcting deviations.

  4. D

    Respond to obstacles

    Detect conflicts and revise motion while maintaining a clear safety boundary.

Research directions

Research directions

  • Integrated route planning and path-following control
  • Obstacle-aware autonomous navigation on an electric-wheelchair platform
  • A practical basis for studying moving obstacles and shared environments
Next step

Sophia University's 2024 faculty feature identifies moving-obstacle handling as the next research objective for the wheelchair platform.
