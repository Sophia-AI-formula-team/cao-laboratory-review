Research area

EV Energy Delivery and Mobility Planning

04

Planning and control for electric vehicles that deliver energy and support mobility in remote or disaster-affected areas.

Status
Active research
Period
Ongoing
Main topics
Electric vehicles · Route optimization · Smart mobility

Overview

Research interests

This research treats electric vehicles as both transportation and mobile energy resources. This research examines route planning for electricity delivery and mobility planning for smart satellite cities.

Problem we are addressing

Energy demand, travel time, vehicle capacity, and road conditions all affect which households or facilities can be served and in what order.

Approach

Research approach

  1. A

    Estimate demand

    Represent where electricity is needed and how demand changes across households or facilities.

  2. B

    Model constraints

    Account for vehicle energy, delivery capacity, road conditions, and service priorities.

  3. C

    Optimize routes

    Use mathematical optimization to decide depot placement, routing, and service order.

  4. D

    Evaluate service

    Compare plans by coverage, resilience, travel cost, and the electricity delivered.

Research directions

Research directions

  • Route-planning methods for EV-based electricity delivery
  • Mobility planning for automated EV services
  • Control and optimization models for resilient local energy supply
Next step

Current work includes traffic-aware routing and planning that considers predicted residential electricity demand.
