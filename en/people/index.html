Department of Engineering and Applied Sciences, Faculty of Science and Technology, Sophia University

I am an Associate Professor at Sophia University. I study how mathematical models and optimization can support safe, efficient motion and energy use. My interests include model predictive control, cooperative driving, powertrain control, and autonomous mobile robots.

Before joining Sophia University in 2018, I worked at Nissan Motor Co., Ltd. I received my Ph.D. from Kyushu University and my master's and bachelor's degrees in engineering from Tianjin University.
