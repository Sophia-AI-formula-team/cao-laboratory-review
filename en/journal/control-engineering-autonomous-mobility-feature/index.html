Sophia feature: applying optimization to autonomous mobility

A university interview introduces Wenjing Cao's research in autonomous wheelchairs, vehicle merging, powertrain control, and EV energy delivery.

Date
1 August 2024
Prepared by
Sophia University
Reading time
3 min read

The university feature explains control engineering through concrete mobility and energy applications in the Cao Laboratory.

Autonomous mobile robots

The feature describes an electric-wheelchair platform that can plan a route to a destination, follow the route, and avoid obstacles. Handling moving obstacles is identified as a subsequent objective.

Vehicles and energy delivery

The same interview outlines research on automated highway merging, automotive powertrain control, and EV-based electricity delivery for communities with different demand conditions.

Key points

  • Control engineering is tested through simulation and physical systems.
  • The laboratory connects autonomous mobility with practical social needs.
  • The official feature is the source for the reported platform status.
